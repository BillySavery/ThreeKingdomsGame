Today's annals

How to play. Each day brings five deaths and battles from the era. Put them in the order they happened, earliest at the top. Right answers lock in and show their year, and you get three checks in all. One placed right on the first check scores a point, on the second half a point and on the third a quarter. Three of the five are spread across the years and two are close together, so watch out for those.

Where the dates come from. Deaths only include people whose year of death is recorded in the historical records, such as the Records of the Three Kingdoms, using the year given on Wikipedia. Battles use the year they're known by on Wikipedia. Two things from the same year never appear on the same day, and a battle that ran into a second year, or a death the records place either side of new year, is never set beside anything just one year away.
