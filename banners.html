Sort the sixteen into four banners of four.

Daily

Banners raised

How to play. Pick four tiles that share a link and submit. Get a group right and its banner is raised. You can make seven mistakes. After five mistakes you can take a hint, which shows three of the four tiles in the easiest banner you have left. Watch for red herrings: some tiles look like they fit more than one group, but only one way solves the whole board. Colours run from easiest to trickiest.
