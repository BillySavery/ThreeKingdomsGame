    How to play. Officers of the era were known by a courtesy name as well as their given name. Each day shows five courtesy names. Name the officer behind each one, with two guesses a name; a miss reveals their faction. The first three are well known figures, the last two go deeper. Every name is checked against Wikipedia, which cites the historical records, and courtesy names shared by more than one person are left out.
