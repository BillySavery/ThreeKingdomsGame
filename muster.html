Today's thirty

How to play. Sixteen squares of factions, battles and events. Thirty officers are called up one at a time. Put each one on a square they belong to, or send them away. Fill the board before the thirty run out.

Every officer can only be used once and every square takes one officer. Some officers fit nothing on today's board, so watch for the duds. A wrong square loses you that officer. There is always a way to fill all sixteen.

Tiger Tally. Once per game you can play the Tiger Tally on the officer in front of you. They fill every open square they fit in one go. Play it on a dud and it is wasted, and the officer is lost.

An officer fits a battle or event if they were there on either side, in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms or in the historical records. Factions count anyone who served that side at any point.
