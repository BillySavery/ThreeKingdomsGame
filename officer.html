    How to play. Guess the mystery officer in 15 tries. Each guess shows how it compares with the answer on faction, gender, home province, birth year, death year and family name. Home is the Han province of the officer's home commandery as the records give it, and amber means a neighbouring province. Arrows on the years point towards the answer: ↑ means the answer was born or died later, ↓ earlier. Amber means within 5 years. A year marked c. is approximate, and ? means no reliable birth year is recorded; a green ? means today's officer has no recorded birth year either. Factions follow the Dynasty Warriors games, so the Sima clan and their allies count as Jin. Warlords, the Han court and the Yellow Turbans count as Other. After 10 guesses you can take an optional hint: something they're famous for, or for lesser known officers, one of the answers you haven't matched yet.
