    How to play. Fill each square with an officer who fits both its row and its column. You have twelve guesses for the nine squares, a wrong name uses one up, and each officer can only be used once. When you finish you'll see how rare your answers were: the share of players who picked the same officer for that square. Lower is better, and an empty square counts as 100. You'll also see your rank against everyone who has finished so far. It moves as more people play, so come back later in the day to see where you end up.

    Where the answers come from. Only people from the historical records count, not characters who only appear in the novel. Wei, Shu and Wu mean the side each officer is best remembered for, and "Not Wei, Shu or Wu" covers everyone else of the era, such as the Han court, Dong Zhuo, Yuan Shao and Liu Biao, plus the Jin dynasty. Home province is the Han province of their home commandery around 190. Death squares only count people whose year of death is recorded exactly. General, Strategist and Ruler go by the one main role each person has in the site's officer list, and a wrong guess always shows the record it was checked against. All of it comes from Wikipedia's lists of people of the Three Kingdoms, which cite the Records of the Three Kingdoms and the Book of the Later Han.
