      Full ranks!

      How to play. Each board has ten slots. Name answers that fit the question. A wrong answer costs a life, and you have three. Only names from the database count, so pick from the dropdown; typos and repeats never cost you a life. Fill all ten slots to complete the ranks. Ranked boards place each answer in its own slot. Some boards have more than ten correct answers, and any ten will do.
