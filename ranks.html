      Full ranks!

      How to play. Each board has ten slots. Name answers that fit the question. A wrong answer costs a life, and you have three. Only names from the database count, so pick from the dropdown; typos and repeats never cost you a life. Fill all ten slots to complete the ranks. Ranked boards place each answer in its own slot. Some boards have more than ten correct answers, and any ten will do. The gold label above each question says what the answers are based on: the historical records, the Romance of the Three Kingdoms novel, the Dynasty Warriors games, or a mix.
