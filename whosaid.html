How to play. Each day brings three lines spoken in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms: one easy, one medium and one hard. Pick who said each one from four names. You get one guess per quote, and the answer comes with the original Chinese line, the chapter and the scene.

Where the quotes come from. Every line is taken from the Chinese text of the novel (the Mao Zonggang edition of 120 chapters), and the speaker is the one the novel names. The English translations were written for this game, so they may read a little differently from the version on your shelf.
